## 2. What Sonata is

A Solana launchpad where a creator launches a community token that trades against a tokenized stock rather than against SOL or USDC. It uses Meteora's Dynamic Bonding Curve (DBC) for launch and price discovery, and graduates liquidity into a Meteora DAMM v2 pool when a curve completes. Trading fees accrue in the stock token. The target user is a memecoin-literate trader, not an issuer or enterprise.

A community token paired with a stock token is not backed equity and conveys no claim on the stock.

Since the 24 September fee-model revision a creator picks one of three fee models at launch (§5.9). The trading fee defaults to 1.25%; Meteora keeps 20% of it (0.25% of volume), and Sonata takes half of the rest (0.5% of volume):

- **Standard token** (the default, Standard mode): the other half, 0.5% of volume, goes to the creator's payout wallet.
- **Reward token** (Standard mode, paid to Sonata's payout bot): that half is paid to the token's holders, pro rata, in the stock. There is no transfer tax and no separate creator fee.
- **Backed token** (StandardFloor mode, the program's **Stock Floor**): 0.25% of volume to the creator and 0.25% into a floor held by the treasury program, which any holder can redeem by burning tokens and the creator can never withdraw (§5.5).

At graduation the locked liquidity is split 50/50, and the creator owns a permanently locked position whose fees they claim (§5.9). Markets launched before the revision keep the mode they were registered in: Refrain (all net fees to the creator, 80% of the trading fee, §5.3), Duet or Floor.

Product flow: **Launch → Trade → Earn.** Demand and sustainable returns are hypotheses. They are not demonstrated by the Devnet test traffic recorded here, and no APR, yield or TVL figure in this project is derived from real usage.

## 5. How a launch works, with Meteora references

### 5.1 One DBC config per launch

Meteora documents that configs are permissionless and that the config, not the pool, fixes who can claim: "Any payer can create a config account, but the config fixes the `fee_claimer` and `leftover_receiver`" ([Meteora — Accounts and permissions](https://docs.meteora.ag/core-products/dbc/accounts-and-permissions)). It also states that "A launchpad or partner defines the quote mint, curve, fees, token type, migration target, and liquidity distribution" ([Meteora — What is DBC](https://docs.meteora.ag/core-products/dbc/what-is-dbc)). Graduation targets and fee tiers are therefore each launchpad's product choice, not Meteora requirements.

Until 22 September every Sonata launch reused the legacy config `CUeJ6fgs…`. Now `prepareLaunch` generates a fresh config keypair per launch ([`runtime.ts:693`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/130b5e206e60800ac721b1dd59cd718d6cc5ab4e/lib/treasury/runtime.ts#L693)) and sends Meteora's `partner.createConfigAndPool` in one transaction ([`runtime.ts:741`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/130b5e206e60800ac721b1dd59cd718d6cc5ab4e/lib/treasury/runtime.ts#L741)), naming the Sonata vault PDA as both `fee_claimer` and `leftover_receiver`. Since the fee-model revision it calls the SDK's `partner.createConfigAndPoolWithFirstBuy` instead, which returns the config and the pool (with the optional first buy) as two transactions ([`runtime.ts:1023-1041`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/68637bb1d3ffecfc3f71d7b93671305d93c56882/lib/treasury/runtime.ts#L1023-L1041), §5.9). This uses a documented Meteora path; it is not a capability Sonata unlocked.

No program redeploy was needed. At registration the treasury program validates the config it is handed rather than pinning an address: `pool.creator == creator`, `pool.config == config`, `pool.base_mint == base_mint`, `config.quote_mint == quote_mint`, `config.fee_claimer == vault` ([`stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs:83-107`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/019c688e4083cfc877fb8a8cb78c8519b92efd8e/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L83-L107)). The only hard-coded public key in that file is `declare_id!` at line 7. Since 23 September those lines are byte-verified against the deployed program (§6.1). The behavioural evidence agrees: the program accepted registration against a new config both before the upgrade, in [`2gvdw2GR…xL6NZMGZ`](https://explorer.solana.com/tx/2gvdw2GRUHHEHt1rYK2Q1AgqtTsMsCtfp5JTbkR5ZGiQnN6ZjcrYKCtkKTUBYRyuTA41QMPLW3Mw5ZoNxL6NZMGZ?cluster=devnet), and after it, in [`2eeR8ASt…b85uCc9Q`](https://explorer.solana.com/tx/2eeR8AStzus18m7Lnus5x4PEM6spMxXk2q1Nb1Sg5ssUSjoGrKeseJAWfYfLH4wD8TJ8F61LhZCzmPVdb85uCc9Q?cluster=devnet).

`initialMarketCap` and `migrationMarketCap` are SDK inputs, not on-chain fields. Meteora's SDK reference describes `buildCurveWithMarketCap` as building a "config from market-cap targets" ([Meteora — TypeScript SDK reference](https://docs.meteora.ag/developer-guides/dbc/typescript-sdk/reference)). The quantity a reviewer can check on-chain is `migrationQuoteThreshold`, recorded in §4.

### 5.3 Where trading fees go

1. Meteora charges the trading fee on each swap. Meteora's protocol "Receives 20% of the total trading fee" ([Meteora — DBC fees](https://docs.meteora.ag/core-products/dbc/fees/overview)).
2. Sonata sets `creatorTradingFeePercentage` to 0, and Meteora states that in that case "the partner receives the full partner and creator fee allocation" (same page). The partner is the config's `fee_claimer`: the Sonata vault PDA.
3. `stockroom_treasury::claim` moves that share into a token account owned by the per-pool treasury PDA (§6.4).
4. `distribute` splits what has been claimed by the mode fixed at registration ([`lib.rs:218-231`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/b1fcb6181babbadc6876c5911320b732ce75b62a/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L218-L231)), paying the `payout_owner` fixed at registration:
   - **Refrain**, the app's default since 24 September: 100% paid out, nothing retained (`Mode::Refrain => 10_000` basis points, [`lib.rs:27-34`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/b1fcb6181babbadc6876c5911320b732ce75b62a/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L27-L34)).
   - **Duet**: 50% paid out and 50% retained as a creator reserve the creator can withdraw. Until 24 September the app registered launches without the Stock Floor in Duet; the flagship and the 2 → 18 proof pools are Duet.
   - **Floor**: 50% paid out and 50% retained as the Stock Floor, which only holders can redeem (§5.5).

   The two modes added on 24 September, which new launches use, are split by `distribute_split` instead, which also pays Sonata's share ([`lib.rs:305-376`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/f6a52efdfcdfcbd453205c00ecfac25996354c63/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L305-L376), §5.9):
   - **Standard**: 50% to the payout owner, 50% to Sonata.
   - **StandardFloor**: 25% to the payout owner, 25% retained as the Stock Floor, 50% to Sonata.

For the default 1.25% fee in Standard mode that is 0.25% of each trade to Meteora's protocol, 0.5% to the creator's payout wallet and 0.5% to Sonata; in StandardFloor mode, 0.25% to Meteora, 0.25% to the creator, 0.25% into the floor and 0.5% to Sonata. For a 1% fee in Refrain mode it is 0.2% of each trade to Meteora's protocol and 0.8% to the creator's payout wallet. For a 3% fee in Duet or Floor mode it is 0.6% to Meteora's protocol and 2.4% to the Sonata vault, then 1.2% paid out and 1.2% retained.

Observed on a 1% Refrain pool (§4): a buy of 5,000,000 atoms (0.05 mQQQ) was charged 50,000 in fees, exactly 1%. Meteora's protocol took 10,000 (20%) and 40,000 accrued to the vault. The treasury claimed all 40,000 in [`41TSSzwK…WV1DC3NC`](https://explorer.solana.com/tx/41TSSzwKP4MRYoYd9FWkUDwFBYrhgw2gxrWR1jmhkySGzMRSzRuGoMDkFbe27YjMtW1ywmhwRNXmgvoWV1DC3NC?cluster=devnet) and paid all 40,000 to the payout owner in [`EwaHrCwE…pRqoS1bD`](https://explorer.solana.com/tx/EwaHrCwErav81xoirZpALHpY2tBgGkHFGDGhpowMwE8HbxKfst5c5Uy3qt9v4JTXioUMKPhUgyX6BPrpRqoS1bD?cluster=devnet), retaining 0.

Meteora's protocol took 30,000 (20%) and 120,000 accrued to the vault. ### 5.4 Graduation to DAMM v2

Meteora specifies that "New DBC configs and new pools must use DAMM v2", that "DBC requires at least 10% of liquidity to remain locked at day 1 after migration", and that it applies "a fixed 0.2% protocol liquidity migration fee during migration" ([Meteora — Migration and liquidity](https://docs.meteora.ag/core-products/dbc/migration-and-liquidity)).

Sonata configs lock all migrated liquidity permanently, well above that floor: until the fee-model revision 100% of it under the partner (the Vault); since then 50% under the Vault and 50% under the creator (§5.9). Consequently no party, including the Sonata vault and the creator, can withdraw migrated LP principal.

**Migration has been executed once, on Devnet** (§4), on per-launch pool [`3HL7AdmR…cap9JNUq`](https://explorer.solana.com/address/3HL7AdmRt8J4p25v61GSkBmkFX7i1SKN5xWbcap9JNUq?cluster=devnet) rather than the flagship, whose curve stays open for the live trading demo. `scripts/verify-graduation.mjs` runs it in the order that matters:

1. A `PartialFill` buy completes the curve. Of 480,000,000 atoms offered, 463,917,526 were used: 450,000,000 into the curve, exactly the migration threshold, plus the 3% fee. The rest was returned. With no vesting configured, the completing swap moves the pool straight to the migration-ready state; there is no locker step ([Meteora DBC `process_swap.rs` at `f552f20`](https://github.com/MeteoraAg/dynamic-bonding-curve/blob/f552f20aa3c1c7631427c3827aeea7c58b902813/programs/dynamic-bonding-curve/src/instructions/swap/process_swap.rs)).
2. Partner fees are claimed and allocated while the pool is still a DBC pool.
3. `migrateToDammV2` is called with the DAMM v2 config for the pool's migration fee option, `FixedBps100`: [`Hv8Lmzmn…Xz8RXcjp`](https://explorer.solana.com/address/Hv8Lmzmnju6m7kcokVKvwqz7QPmdX9XfKjJsXz8RXcjp?cluster=devnet), whose pool-creator authority is the DBC pool authority, as migration requires. Meteora's separate `migration_damm_v2_create_metadata` instruction is deprecated and does nothing at this revision, so it is not called ([source](https://github.com/MeteoraAg/dynamic-bonding-curve/blob/f552f20aa3c1c7631427c3827aeea7c58b902813/programs/dynamic-bonding-curve/src/instructions/migration/dynamic_amm_v2/migration_damm_v2_create_metadata.rs)).

**Limit, stated plainly:** the Vault's locked position earns DAMM v2 trading fees, but it is owned by the vault PDA and `stockroom_treasury` has no instruction to claim DAMM v2 position fees. Measured after the one trade above, the position already held 7,999 atoms of unclaimed mSPY fees — 0.8% of the 1,000,000-atom input, consistent with the 1% `FixedBps100` pool fee less the protocol share — read with the DAMM v2 SDK's `getUnClaimLpFee`. Those fees accrue and cannot currently be collected; on the creator-position proof the Vault's position likewise holds 39,999 unclaimed atoms (§5.9). Closing that needs a treasury program change.

### 5.5 Stock Floor

A creator can choose the Stock Floor at launch; it is never the default. Since the fee-model revision it is the app's **Backed token** fee model, registered in StandardFloor mode; before it, the "Stock Floor" option, registered in Floor mode (§5.2). It is fixed at registration: `init_treasury` writes the mode once and no instruction changes it (subject to §6.2).

- **What goes in.** In Floor mode `distribute` pays 50% of each claimed batch to the payout owner and retains 50%, as Duet does ([`lib.rs:32`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/39883450bb2f2e5c0d068c3e478b3c506e3e6717/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L32)). With a 3% fee that is 1.2% of every trade into the floor, after Meteora's 20% share. In StandardFloor mode `distribute_split` retains 25% of each batch as the floor, pays 25% to the payout owner and 50% to Sonata ([`lib.rs:52-53`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/f6a52efdfcdfcbd453205c00ecfac25996354c63/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L52-L53)): 0.25% of every trade into the floor at the default 1.25% fee. Sonata's share is booked as retained and withdrawn in the same instruction, so the floor is still retained − withdrawn.
- **Who can take it out: holders, by burning.** `redeem(amount)` burns `amount` of the community token from the signer's own account and pays `floor × amount ÷ supply` of the quote stock, rounded down, to the signer's own account ([`lib.rs:274-334`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/39883450bb2f2e5c0d068c3e478b3c506e3e6717/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L274-L334)). `floor` is the treasury's retained balance not yet paid out; `supply` is the base mint's supply read in the same instruction. It accepts Floor and StandardFloor treasuries ([`lib.rs:63-66`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/f6a52efdfcdfcbd453205c00ecfac25996354c63/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L63-L66), checked at [`:428`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/f6a52efdfcdfcbd453205c00ecfac25996354c63/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L428)).
- **The creator cannot.** `withdraw_retained` fails with `FloorLocked` on a Floor treasury ([`lib.rs:234`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/39883450bb2f2e5c0d068c3e478b3c506e3e6717/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L234)) and on a StandardFloor treasury ([`lib.rs:381`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/f6a52efdfcdfcbd453205c00ecfac25996354c63/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L381)). The rewards program funds campaigns through that same instruction, so it cannot draw from a floor either.
- **Nobody else's share goes down.** The payout rounds down, so a redemption never lowers the floor per remaining token: (F − p) ÷ (S − a) ≥ F ÷ S whenever p ≤ F·a ÷ S. The Devnet proof checks this after a real redemption (`floorPerTokenNotReduced`), and the app flow shows the floor per 1M tokens unchanged after a burn (§4).
- **Conservative by construction.** `supply` counts every token in existence, including unsold tokens still on the curve and, after graduation, tokens inside the locked DAMM v2 position. Their share stays in the treasury: tokens on the curve carry it to whoever buys them, and the locked position's share can never be redeemed. The floor per token a holder sees is therefore a lower bound.
- **Why it holds.** If the market price falls below the floor per token, buying and burning is profitable, which pushes the price back up. That is a mechanism, not a price guarantee: the floor per token starts small and grows with trading volume.

**Limit:** the floor grows only from DBC trading fees, so it stops growing at graduation, because the Vault's locked DAMM v2 position's fees are not claimable (§5.4). Redemption keeps working after graduation. The StandardFloor variant has been exercised only in the 18 local tests of `tests/treasury-split.test.mjs`, not yet on Devnet (§3).

Proven on Devnet (§4) by `scripts/verify-stock-floor.mjs`, which asserts eleven checks against chain state, then again through the app. In the app every Floor market shows the floor, what new trades will add, and a burn action ([`stock-floor.tsx`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/ca12d2d489f85f0a7eb8341d3b2c6d94ec8323a4/app/onchain/stock-floor.tsx)); "Add to floor" sends `claim` and `distribute` in one transaction ([`runtime.ts:360`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/ca12d2d489f85f0a7eb8341d3b2c6d94ec8323a4/lib/treasury/runtime.ts#L360)), and the burn is built at [`runtime.ts:371`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/ca12d2d489f85f0a7eb8341d3b2c6d94ec8323a4/lib/treasury/runtime.ts#L371) using the program's rounding ([`floor.ts:7`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/ca12d2d489f85f0a7eb8341d3b2c6d94ec8323a4/lib/treasury/floor.ts#L7)).

This is a Sonata mechanism in the treasury program. Meteora's documentation is not a source for it; the only Meteora behaviour it relies on is the existing fee path (§5.3, §6.4).

### 5.6 Token profile

Like pump.fun's create form, a launch can carry an image, a short description and Website, X and Telegram links. They go into a Metaplex-style metadata JSON, with the links both under `extensions` and as top-level `website`, `twitter` and `telegram`, and its URI is passed as `uri` in `createConfigAndPool`'s `preCreatePoolParam`. It is written into the token's metadata account at creation, and the token's authority is immutable (§5.2), so the profile cannot be changed afterwards.

- **Storage.** Since 23 September, Sonata's S3 bucket behind CloudFront. Each file is stored at `tokens/<sha256>.<ext>`, where the hash is of the file's own bytes ([`s3-upload.ts`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/e2041fe76a1cd992d9148a94e634c14268893ce3/lib/server/s3-upload.ts)), so anyone can check that the image or JSON behind a token's URI is the one uploaded. The key is kept short because the URI is written into the launch transaction, which Solana caps at 1,232 bytes. For the same reason the launch carries no compute-unit instruction: it uses about 140,000 units, under the default budget, and a size check refuses a launch that would still exceed the cap ([`runtime.ts`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/e2041fe76a1cd992d9148a94e634c14268893ce3/lib/treasury/runtime.ts)). Checked on a real launch (§4): its [metadata](https://d3lwm4c3ge2mv2.cloudfront.net/tokens/5bafeeaefe7a1f1bae09c7d30ffd0a249901ca2495458667ba935fa43e86905a.json) and [image](https://d3lwm4c3ge2mv2.cloudfront.net/tokens/237c9ec84dfff2b32c37e153984bfd222721288f213607d7c78e6b32dab1f54b.webp) hash to their names, and serve with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *` and a one-year immutable cache. The app itself reads profiles through its own `/api/token-meta` ([`route.ts`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/e2041fe76a1cd992d9148a94e634c14268893ce3/app/api/token-meta/route.ts)), which fetches only Sonata's CloudFront and Irys URIs, sends a User-Agent (CloudFront's managed firewall rejects requests without one), caps the size and returns only validated fields, so the page does not depend on a cross-site fetch. The server's AWS key can only add files under `tokens/`. The bucket owner could still delete or replace an object; a changed file would then no longer match its name. Launches before that date, and any setup without S3 settings, use Irys devnet, Arweave's test network: uploads under about 100 KB are free, so the server signs each upload with a throwaway key and holds no secret. The uploader builds ANS-104 data items with WebCrypto ([`irys-upload.ts:104`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/ca12d2d489f85f0a7eb8341d3b2c6d94ec8323a4/lib/server/irys-upload.ts#L104)); its output was compared byte for byte with the official `@irys/bundles` library, and `irys-upload.test.ts` pins that result. **Devnet uploads are temporary;** production would use permanent Arweave storage.
- **Validation.** Links must be `https`, with no credentials; X links must be on x.com or twitter.com and Telegram links on t.me or telegram.me ([`token-profile.ts:24`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/ca12d2d489f85f0a7eb8341d3b2c6d94ec8323a4/lib/token-profile.ts#L24)). Images are checked by their first bytes (PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF only), square-cropped and re-encoded under 95 KB in the browser. When a profile is read back, only two kinds of URI are fetched: Sonata's CloudFront, at `tokens/<sha256>.<ext>`, and Irys ([`token-profile.ts:72-85`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/980cd485697ad2562e1c5054f845fef676825842/lib/token-profile.ts#L72-L85), enforced by [`token-meta/route.ts:18`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/980cd485697ad2562e1c5054f845fef676825842/app/api/token-meta/route.ts#L18)), and every link is validated again before display ([`token-profile.ts:125-157`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/980cd485697ad2562e1c5054f845fef676825842/lib/token-profile.ts#L125-L157)); a token whose metadata points elsewhere shows no image or links.
- **Proven through the app** (§4). On 23 September the metadata account of the first app launch (pool [`EFMUmeNJ…GJtMHUwQ`](https://explorer.solana.com/address/EFMUmeNJcz8Z49c74sTrmKcPq3qQsdchjzjGGJtMHUwQ?cluster=devnet)) held its Irys URI, the JSON resolved with the description and website, and the 4,570-byte WebP image resolved. That profile no longer displays: Irys devnet now answers those URIs with an HTTP 307 redirect to another host (checked 24 September), and `/api/token-meta` does not follow redirects ([`route.ts:22-28`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/980cd485697ad2562e1c5054f845fef676825842/app/api/token-meta/route.ts#L22-L28)). The later launch's metadata JSON on CloudFront (pool [`fbAaZr9z…mh618f3y`](https://explorer.solana.com/address/fbAaZr9zJAFARc8cLyEvhi5kyJyrP1A969Nmh618f3y?cluster=devnet)) still returned HTTP 200 on 24 September.

### 5.7 Market data: the trade indexer

Charts need history the chain does not index for us. `indexer/` ([`indexer`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/tree/f3ec32217f1c8a6a8386d41bbf023ba63bfd7b5b/indexer)) runs beside the app on the same server:

- **Discovery.** Every pool with a Sonata treasury account is a market.
- **Reading trades.** For each pool it pages through the pool's signatures and decodes Meteora DBC's swap event from each transaction's inner instructions ([`parse.mjs:36`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/f3ec32217f1c8a6a8386d41bbf023ba63bfd7b5b/indexer/parse.mjs#L36)). The event carries the amounts, the fees and the post-trade square-root price, so nothing is inferred from balance changes. DBC's `swap2` emits both the legacy `evtSwap` and `evtSwap2` for one trade; only `evtSwap2` is kept. The parser is tested against a real Devnet trade (`indexer/parse.test.mjs`).
- **Storage.** PostgreSQL on the server's localhost: one row per trade (signature, side, trader, amounts, fee, price, time).
- **Serving.** A read-only API at `/api/index/*` (candles, recent trades, 24h stats): GET only, validated inputs, a per-client rate limit, and parameterised queries.
- **Limits.** It polls the public Devnet RPC every 20 seconds, one transaction at a time to stay under its rate limit; high-volume platforms stream transactions from an RPC provider instead. Trades on a graduated market's DAMM v2 pool are not indexed yet. The data is for display only: nothing in it feeds a transaction.

### 5.9 The launch model from 24 September

Revised on 24 September, after the one-page launch: Sonata takes a share of the fee, the creator can buy first, a launch takes one approval, creators keep earning after graduation, and a token can pay its holders instead of its creator. Markets launched earlier keep the mode they were registered in, and the program serves them unchanged.

**Fee model and Sonata's share.** Per trade, at the default 1.25% fee (2% and 3% scale the same way; a Reward token at 3% pays holders 1.2%, the picker's second rate):

| App's fee model | Treasury mode | Payout owner | Creator | Holders | Sonata | Meteora |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard token (default) | Standard | creator's payout wallet | 0.5% | — | 0.5% | 0.25% |
| Reward token | Standard | the payout bot | — | 0.5%, paid out | 0.5% | 0.25% |
| Backed token (Stock Floor) | StandardFloor | creator's payout wallet | 0.25% | 0.25%, as the floor | 0.5% | 0.25% |

- **Program.** `Standard` and `StandardFloor` are appended to `Mode`, so every existing treasury decodes unchanged ([`lib.rs:24-31`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/f6a52efdfcdfcbd453205c00ecfac25996354c63/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L24-L31)). The new permissionless `distribute_split` ([`lib.rs:305-376`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/f6a52efdfcdfcbd453205c00ecfac25996354c63/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L305-L376)) splits what is claimed but unallocated: the creator share (rounded down) goes to the payout owner's token account, the floor share (rounded down) stays in custody, and Sonata takes the rest, rounding included, paid to a token account whose owner must be the Vault's recorded admin ([`lib.rs:622`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/f6a52efdfcdfcbd453205c00ecfac25996354c63/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L622)). In the tests, 1,001 atoms split 500 / 0 / 501 in Standard and 250 / 250 / 501 in StandardFloor.
- **Accounting.** The creator share is recorded as distributed; the floor and Sonata's share as retained; and Sonata's share also as withdrawn, in the same instruction. So `total_retained − total_withdrawn` is still exactly the floor (0 in Standard), and custody still covers what is unallocated plus the floor. On the dev-buy market below the treasury reads claimed 1,000,000, distributed 500,000, retained 500,000, withdrawn 500,000.
- **What refuses what.** `distribute` refuses the new modes (`UseDistributeSplit`, [`lib.rs:253-256`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/f6a52efdfcdfcbd453205c00ecfac25996354c63/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L253-L256)) and `distribute_split` the old ones (`UseDistribute`, [`:315`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/f6a52efdfcdfcbd453205c00ecfac25996354c63/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L315)); `withdraw_retained` refuses both new modes (`FloorLocked` for StandardFloor, `NoCreatorReserve` for Standard, [`:381-386`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/f6a52efdfcdfcbd453205c00ecfac25996354c63/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L381-L386)); `redeem` accepts StandardFloor (§5.5). `tests/treasury-split.test.mjs` covers these in 18 tests, including a crank by an unrelated signer, a platform share sent to a non-admin account, a creator share sent elsewhere, and redemptions that try to reach Sonata's or the creator's share.
- **Who receives Sonata's share.** The Vault admin, `vb4pmin…`, which is also the deployer and the upgrade authority (§6.2). No instruction changes the Vault admin.
- **Proven on Devnet** (§4). On the dev-buy launch below, a 1 mQQQ first buy at 1.25% accrued a 1,000,000-atom partner share (80% of the 1,250,000-atom fee); the payout bot's one transaction claimed it and paid 500,000 to the creator's payout wallet and 500,000 to the Vault admin's quote account: [`4e4Vda6T…x3DnAdw1`](https://explorer.solana.com/tx/4e4Vda6Tmu7AJ49toRx7VK3u1g5SEibc1hqW2b3CQuC3xYsNnrTWjtR9nryg5owAGCJ62nckXPNMCnWex3DnAdw1?cluster=devnet). StandardFloor has not yet run on Devnet (§3).

**Dev buy and the one-approval launch.**

- **The first buy.** A creator can buy up to 75% of the supply with the quote stock as the pool's very first trade. `prepareLaunch` quotes it with `buyOut` ([`graduation.ts:92-114`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/68637bb1d3ffecfc3f71d7b93671305d93c56882/lib/treasury/graduation.ts#L92-L114)), DBC's own swap math: the fee is taken from the input first, rounded up, then each curve segment yields L × (b − a) ÷ (a × b) base tokens, rounded down. It refuses a buy above 75% or beyond the whole curve, or one the wallet cannot pay for, and sets the minimum out to 99.5% of the quote ([`runtime.ts:990-1022`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/68637bb1d3ffecfc3f71d7b93671305d93c56882/lib/treasury/runtime.ts#L990-L1022)). `buyOut` has no unit test; it was checked against the SDK's `swapQuote` on an untouched Devnet pool for three amounts and matched.
- **Nothing trades first.** The buy is made with Meteora's `createConfigAndPoolWithFirstBuy`, so the swap is in the transaction that creates the pool ([`runtime.ts:1023-1041`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/68637bb1d3ffecfc3f71d7b93671305d93c56882/lib/treasury/runtime.ts#L1023-L1041)). **Creators keep earning after graduation.**

- **How.** Configs now split the locked DAMM v2 liquidity 50/50 (§5.2). - **Proven on Devnet** by `scripts/verify-creator-position.mjs`, which uses the app's own `creator-position.ts` ([`artifacts/creator-position-proof.json`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/f6a52efdfcdfcbd453205c00ecfac25996354c63/artifacts/creator-position-proof.json), 20 checks, all true): DBC pool [`51fyqN7f…aefKrecR`](https://explorer.solana.com/address/51fyqN7fdXAdRMagYya92cQ7caSP5FUDJpQvaefKrecR?cluster=devnet) graduated to DAMM v2 pool [`3Ye4dSic…PNeeaaXV`](https://explorer.solana.com/address/3Ye4dSicqEiceZeHFvcbKTMSiiti7nJtf7o2PNeeaaXV?cluster=devnet) with exactly two positions, each permanently locked and each holding half the liquidity; after the trade the creator claimed 39,999 atoms of mQQQ in [`54VLGGUZ…KYejx6UU`](https://explorer.solana.com/tx/54VLGGUZoYkSwRGp4ZFmsd65FBMjhJ79cgfTv6mzRGMj7jtGUiWn5JWXipywt2fU5sjySdYMboaUPMvKKYejx6UU?cluster=devnet), and the position stayed fully locked. **Reward tokens and the payout bot.**

- **What it is.** A Reward token is a Standard-mode market whose payout owner is the payout bot's key [`Fb83XLPd…UzP8dtGz`](https://explorer.solana.com/address/Fb83XLPdUM11FrUUBNaB1JXJ2feJacNkcPGUzP8dtGz?cluster=devnet) ([`runtime.ts:39-43`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/68637bb1d3ffecfc3f71d7b93671305d93c56882/lib/treasury/runtime.ts#L39-L43), set at [`:927`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/68637bb1d3ffecfc3f71d7b93671305d93c56882/lib/treasury/runtime.ts#L927)). `distribute_split` pays the creator's half of net fees to the bot's quote account and Sonata's half to the Vault admin. There is no transfer tax: holders are paid from the trading fee, and the creator is paid only as a holder.
- **How holders are paid.** After each pass's claims and splits, the bot pays what it holds for each Reward token market to that token's holders, pro rata, in the quote token ([`crank.mjs:547-579`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/68637bb1d3ffecfc3f71d7b93671305d93c56882/indexer/crank.mjs#L547-L579), [`indexer/rewards.mjs`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/68637bb1d3ffecfc3f71d7b93671305d93c56882/indexer/rewards.mjs)):
  - Owed per market = the treasury's on-chain `total_distributed` minus what the ledger has paid for that pool ([`rewards.mjs:57-62`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/68637bb1d3ffecfc3f71d7b93671305d93c56882/indexer/rewards.mjs#L57-L62)). Markets with the same quote token share one bot account, so the ledger keeps them apart, and if the bot's balance does not cover everything owed in that token, none of them is paid.
  - Holders: a snapshot of the base mint's token accounts, summed per wallet, excluding the pool's base vault, the treasury, the bot, owners that are not ordinary wallets (program-derived addresses such as pool authorities) and anyone under 0.01% of supply; the top 200 by balance ([`rewards.mjs:64-97`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/68637bb1d3ffecfc3f71d7b93671305d93c56882/indexer/rewards.mjs#L64-L97)).
  - Shares: owed × balance ÷ the holders' total, rounded down ([`rewards.mjs:99-107`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/68637bb1d3ffecfc3f71d7b93671305d93c56882/indexer/rewards.mjs#L99-L107)). Only existing token accounts that can receive are paid; the bot never pays rent to create one. Skipped shares and rounding stay owed for a later pass. Nothing is paid until a market is owed at least 100,000 atoms (0.001 of the stock).
  - Transactions: Token-2022 `transfer_checked`, 20 per transaction (1,210 of 1,232 bytes), each batch simulated first.
  - Ledger: PostgreSQL tables `reward_payouts` (confirmed payouts) and `reward_pending`. A signed payout is written to `reward_pending` before it is sent and counts as paid until the chain settles it, so a crash between sending and recording cannot pay the same holders twice.
  - Served at `GET /api/index/rewards?pool=` ([`index.mjs:208-225`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/68637bb1d3ffecfc3f71d7b93671305d93c56882/indexer/index.mjs#L208-L225)) and shown on the market page as "Holder rewards" ([`treasury-workspace.tsx:46-88`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/68637bb1d3ffecfc3f71d7b93671305d93c56882/app/onchain/treasury-workspace.tsx#L46-L88)). `indexer/rewards.test.mjs` has 10 tests.
- **Proven on Devnet** (§4). The test wallet launched Reward token pool [`EPrY62cT…VAi4gg26`](https://explorer.solana.com/address/EPrY62cTtaougRANiMUX4SYLYwA4UKanehrGVAi4gg26?cluster=devnet) through the page with a 1 mQQQ first buy (127,622,563.43 tokens), and the deployer bought 1 mQQQ (100,455,215.77 tokens). The bot's claim and split moved 2,000,000 atoms, 1,000,000 to Sonata and 1,000,000 to the bot: [`65VrfBGP…TgoZCjNk`](https://explorer.solana.com/tx/65VrfBGPdE39HUhNuNBnHCDmKDZV16FDwcZo49DNY9xpouS8qTBsAHvLMJHW4VYrgLmBeMVKZ4EapWoTTgoZCjNk?cluster=devnet). Five seconds later it paid 559,557 atoms to the test wallet and 440,442 to the deployer, pro rata to 127.62M and 100.46M tokens: [`5SJu3izu…qPHPfX5M`](https://explorer.solana.com/tx/5SJu3izu1WLVMdMK2G9UCfAiYDm2N1mefzmchtpBSpyLzS9gEqzcDH9TEviyJFu3G4MRt6a3uv624rgkqPHPfX5M?cluster=devnet). One atom of rounding stays owed. The hosted API then reported `paid` 999,999 in 1 payout to 2 recipients. Both holders are project wallets, and the deployer is also the Vault admin.
- **Custody, stated plainly.** For Reward tokens the bot's key holds the holders' rewards between `distribute_split` and the payout: a few seconds, or until a later pass when a market is under the threshold or a holder was skipped. That is custodial, as holder payouts made by the platforms of pump.fun, Ember and StonkFun are. Whoever controls the key file on the server controls those funds, and nothing on-chain checks how the bot divides them among holders; only the total it receives is fixed by the program. Each market's payout owner is fixed at registration, so losing or replacing the key would strand every Reward token market registered to it and any rewards still in its account. It is a Devnet key in one file (mode 600) on one server, with no multisig. Standard and Backed tokens are not affected: for them the key can still only pay fees (§5.3).

### 6.2 Upgrade authority: what "immutable" means here

All four programs run under the BPF upgradeable loader with upgrade authority `vb4pminVbRa8BRaRCDa7JmAkFx6LSmnwMiDtsKvkXVF`, which is also the deployer and the flagship market's creator. Where this document says a payout recipient, an allocation or a binding cannot be changed, that is true of every instruction in the current program. The upgrade authority can replace the program. For production the authority would move to a multisig or be revoked; that has not been done.

### 6.3 Authority model

- **Register.** `init_treasury` requires the DBC pool's own creator to sign and binds the treasury to the pool through the five checks in §5.1 ([`lib.rs:76-128`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/019c688e4083cfc877fb8a8cb78c8519b92efd8e/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L76-L128)). The treasury PDA is seeded `["treasury", pool]`.
- **Claim and distribute are permissionless.** Neither `Claim` nor `Distribute` declares a signer ([`lib.rs:306-347`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/019c688e4083cfc877fb8a8cb78c8519b92efd8e/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L306-L347)), so anyone can crank them, and Sonata's payout crank does (§5.3). That is safe because the destinations are pinned: claimed fees can only enter treasury-owned accounts, and `distribute` requires `payout_quote.owner == treasury.payout_owner` ([`lib.rs:343`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/019c688e4083cfc877fb8a8cb78c8519b92efd8e/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L343)).
- **Split with Sonata.** `distribute_split` is permissionless too, and every destination is pinned ([`lib.rs:609-626`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/f6a52efdfcdfcbd453205c00ecfac25996354c63/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L609-L626)): the Vault must be the PDA at `["stockroom"]` and the treasury the PDA at `["treasury", pool]`; custody must be a token account of the quote mint whose authority is the treasury; the creator share goes only to a token account whose owner is `treasury.payout_owner`; Sonata's share only to one whose owner is `vault.admin` (`InvalidPlatformRecipient`). When the payout owner is the Vault admin both are one account, marked `dup`; no token account is serialized by the program, so the alias is harmless. It refuses the older modes, and `distribute` refuses the new ones (§5.9).
- **Withdraw.** `withdraw_retained` requires the creator's signature and sends only to the creator ([`lib.rs:227`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/019c688e4083cfc877fb8a8cb78c8519b92efd8e/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L227), signer at `:268`). It is refused on Floor treasuries ([`lib.rs:234`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/39883450bb2f2e5c0d068c3e478b3c506e3e6717/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L234)), and on both new modes: `FloorLocked` on StandardFloor, `NoCreatorReserve` on Standard, whose retained balance is only Sonata's share, already paid out ([`lib.rs:381-386`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/f6a52efdfcdfcbd453205c00ecfac25996354c63/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L381-L386)).
- **Redeem.** Any holder may call it, but it burns only from a token account whose authority is the signer and pays only to a token account owned by the signer; `base_mint` and `quote_mint` are bound to the treasury by `has_one`, and each token program is bound to its mint ([`lib.rs:337-354`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/39883450bb2f2e5c0d068c3e478b3c506e3e6717/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L337-L354)). `tests/treasury-floor.test.mjs` tries burning another holder's tokens, redirecting the payout, substituting the mint, over-burning and dust amounts; each is refused. Since the fourth upgrade it also accepts StandardFloor treasuries, and `tests/treasury-split.test.mjs` shows a redemption there never reaches the creator's or Sonata's share.
- **Partner metadata.** `create_partner_metadata` requires the Vault's recorded admin to sign (`has_one = admin`, [`lib.rs:417`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/ae49603af2cb8dea0450b3d3a9aef1a6d9e88b60/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L417)); the Vault signs only the DBC call. `tests/treasury-partner-metadata.test.mjs` shows a non-admin call refused.
- **Payout bot key** (`Fb83XLPd…`). For ordinary markets it only pays fees and rent for `claim`, `distribute` and `distribute_split`, none of which needs its signature, so it cannot redirect anything (§5.3). For Reward tokens it is the registered payout owner: it receives the holders' half, holds it until the payout transaction, and divides it among holders off-chain, with nothing on-chain constraining that division. Whoever holds the key controls those funds, and losing it strands those markets' payouts (§5.9).
- **Sonata's recipient.** The Vault admin, recorded by `init_vault`; no instruction changes it, subject to §6.2. It is `vb4pmin…`, the same key as the upgrade authority.
- **Payout recipient.** Set once at registration ([`lib.rs:118`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/019c688e4083cfc877fb8a8cb78c8519b92efd8e/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L118)). No instruction changes it, subject to §6.2.

### 6.4 Fee custody

Until they are distributed, partner fees are never held by a wallet. `claim` calls Meteora DBC's `claim_trading_fee` by CPI, passing the vault PDA as `fee_claimer` and signing with seeds `["stockroom"]` ([`lib.rs:133-155`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/019c688e4083cfc877fb8a8cb78c8519b92efd8e/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L133-L155)). DBC authorises that call only when `config.fee_claimer` equals the signing key ([Meteora DBC `access_control.rs` at the pinned revision `f552f20`](https://github.com/MeteoraAg/dynamic-bonding-curve/blob/f552f20aa3c1c7631427c3827aeea7c58b902813/programs/dynamic-bonding-curve/src/access_control.rs)). The vault's seeds exist only inside the treasury program, so no external key can produce that signature.

Proceeds land in accounts constrained `token::authority = treasury` ([`lib.rs:318-321`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/019c688e4083cfc877fb8a8cb78c8519b92efd8e/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs#L318-L321)). DBC itself does not constrain those destination accounts, so this guarantee rests on Sonata's constraint, not Meteora's.

On-chain: both configs decode to `fee_claimer = leftover_receiver = 5XMFEnW8Ur3EswbFhCr1LEKtHDioTs8oeQEHKyPHNpp5`, which is `PDA(["stockroom"], GPANv5z…)`. The flagship custody account `6xZKeq3b…` is a Token-2022 account whose token authority is the treasury PDA `QZJpgjyu…`.

"Not held by the creator" describes custody, not entitlement. The creator reaches the retained share through `withdraw_retained` (Duet only), and names the payout recipient at registration. After distribution the shares are in wallets: the creator's payout wallet, the Vault admin's for Sonata's share, and, for Reward tokens, the payout bot's until it pays holders (§5.9).

### 6.5 Holder rewards: what is enforced on-chain, and what is trusted

The 18 September handoff called the snapshot "creator-attested/offchain". That understated the implementation. It also needs a sharper boundary than "not a production trustless distributor".

**Enforced by code:**

- Holder discovery is a full token-account scan, never a top-holders sample ([`holder-scan.ts:8-31`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/130b5e206e60800ac721b1dd59cd718d6cc5ab4e/lib/rewards/holder-scan.ts#L8-L31)). On an indexed RPC, completeness depends on the RPC returning every matching account.
- When public RPC has secondary indexes disabled, the fallback returns a holder set only if its balances sum exactly to the mint supply read in the same RPC response, and otherwise refuses: "Public RPC could not prove a complete holder snapshot" ([`holder-scan.ts:32-85`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/130b5e206e60800ac721b1dd59cd718d6cc5ab4e/lib/rewards/holder-scan.ts#L32-L85)).
- The snapshot document is SHA-256 hashed ([`holders.ts:172-184`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/130b5e206e60800ac721b1dd59cd718d6cc5ab4e/lib/rewards/holders.ts#L172-L184)).
- The eight-recipient cap is a protocol limit, not a UI guard: `MAX_MEMBERS = 8` ([`stockroom-rewards/src/lib.rs:12`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/019c688e4083cfc877fb8a8cb78c8519b92efd8e/programs/stockroom-rewards/src/lib.rs#L12)). Clients fail closed rather than truncate.
- `fund` draws only from the treasury's un-withdrawn retained fees, and allocations are written once into a PDA and never edited, cancelled or refunded ([`lib.rs:107-178`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/019c688e4083cfc877fb8a8cb78c8519b92efd8e/programs/stockroom-rewards/src/lib.rs#L107-L178)).
- Double claims are blocked by `claimed_mask` on both `deliver` and `claim`. `deliver` needs no recipient signature but pays only the recorded recipient's account ([`lib.rs:85-105`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/019c688e4083cfc877fb8a8cb78c8519b92efd8e/programs/stockroom-rewards/src/lib.rs#L85-L105)).
- **When the creator runs `record_round`**, it is strict: the preceding instruction in the same transaction must be this program's `fund` on the same campaign; the snapshot must be no more than 300 slots old; and the program recomputes the budget from the treasury's retained total and the policy, rejecting the round unless the funded amount matches exactly ([`lib.rs:58-83`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/019c688e4083cfc877fb8a8cb78c8519b92efd8e/programs/stockroom-rewards/src/lib.rs#L58-L83)).

**Trusted, stated plainly:**

- `record_round` is optional. Nothing downstream requires a round record to exist, so a creator can fund a campaign with no attestation at all.
- The per-recipient split is never checked on-chain. The program stores `snapshot_hash` as given and never recomputes it from the allocations. The hash makes a later substitution detectable; it does not make one impossible.
- The operator that computes rounds runs locally. It is not an always-on service.

Both the rewards and treasury programs' deployed bytes match their source (§6.1), so these citations are byte-verified.

Holder policies are keyed per market (`["holder-policy", treasury]`, [`holders.ts:30-33`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/130b5e206e60800ac721b1dd59cd718d6cc5ab4e/lib/rewards/holders.ts#L30-L33)), so a creator can enable one on any market they created, after registration. Liquidity allocation is not generalised: the liquidity runtime is bound to the single pool `GHHFvUXd…`.

Reward tokens (§5.9) are a separate mechanism: they do not use the rewards program, have no eight-recipient cap (the payout bot pays up to 200 holders), and their per-holder split is computed by the bot off-chain; only the total the bot receives is fixed on-chain.

### 6.6 Client-side checks are not the security boundary

`validateMarketIdentity` ([`runtime.ts:539-555`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/130b5e206e60800ac721b1dd59cd718d6cc5ab4e/lib/treasury/runtime.ts#L539-L555)) pins the program and vault to values bundled with the frontend and checks the quote mint against the bundled registry. A substituted frontend controls those inputs, so this is a usability guard. The binding checks are the program's registration constraints (§5.1) and `readTreasury`, which re-reads the treasury, pool and config from chain and rejects any mismatch before a transaction is offered for signing ([`runtime.ts:81-136`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/130b5e206e60800ac721b1dd59cd718d6cc5ab4e/lib/treasury/runtime.ts#L81-L136)).

### 6.7 Reliance on behaviour Meteora does not document

Meteora documents fee claims as well suited to CPI — "Account lists are smaller and signer roles are clear" ([Meteora — Rust CPI](https://docs.meteora.ag/developer-guides/dbc/rust-integration/cpi)) — and describes `fee_claimer` as the "Partner authority allowed to claim partner fees" ([Meteora — Program accounts](https://docs.meteora.ag/developer-guides/dbc/program/accounts)).

What Meteora does not state is that `fee_claimer` may be a PDA owned by a third-party program. Its docs are silent on that case, not prohibitive. Sonata's entire fee path depends on it. It works on Devnet by demonstration (§4), not by documented guarantee, so a future DBC release could tighten the signer check without that counting as a documented breaking change. Mitigation per Meteora's own guidance: pin the IDL and program versions. The treasury pins DBC at `f552f20` in `programs/stockroom-treasury/Cargo.toml`.

## 7. Mainnet gap: stock tokens need a Meteora token badge

Meteora: "Token 2022 quote assets are permissionless only when the mint uses metadata-related extensions and has a zero transfer fee." Mints with other extensions need an operator-created token badge, and Meteora states: "This is the path used for Stock Tokens and other Token 2022 quote assets that need extra extensions" ([Meteora — Token 2022 support](https://docs.meteora.ag/core-products/dbc/token-2022-support)).

Devnet mSPY ([`6gat24pu…ZHtgNJqg`](https://explorer.solana.com/address/6gat24puM23p74CeBKEPs53roxqpHcQpiGL8ZHtgNJqg?cluster=devnet)) is Token-2022 with 8 decimals, no freeze authority, and exactly two extensions: `MetadataPointer` and `TokenMetadata` (read from chain, 23 September). It therefore uses the permissionless path. **The Devnet proof does not demonstrate the token-badge path that real stock tokens use.** Sonata's `createConfigAndPool` call passes no badge.

## 8. Context: other products on Meteora DBC

Third-party figures below are point-in-time readings of public pages, not audited and not reconciled on-chain.

**StockLaunch.** Per Solana Compass, "StockLaunch is a launchpad built on top of Meteora's Dynamic Bonding Curve". Creators select one of twenty Backpack-issued equities as the quote asset instead of USDC or SOL, can configure trading fees from 0% to 50%, and fees accumulate in a reward vault in the chosen stock token, distributed to holders on a time-weighted basis ([Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/news/meteora-opens-token-launches-paired-with-backpack-issued-stocks-via-stocklaunch)). It is live on mainnet with real, redeemable stock tokens. Sonata is on Devnet with a mock stock token, and its holder distribution is creator-funded rounds capped at eight recipients rather than continuous time-weighted accrual. We have not inspected StockLaunch's contracts.

**Embercurve.** Read on 22–23 September ([embercurve.fun/launch](https://embercurve.fun/launch)): every coin opens at a fixed $4,000 market cap; creators choose one of three graduation presets ($25K, $35K, $40K), one of three fee tiers (1%, 2%, 3%) and one of four curve shapes. When read, Ember's aggregate metrics displayed no values and its pair registry was empty, which is one reason to treat all third-party numbers as point-in-time. Ember also gates quote assets at the platform level, as Sonata does with its registry. Read again on 24 September on the same page: by default a "Holder rewards" module pays the top 2,000 holders every 30 minutes from Ember's wallet, the creator receives $0 by default, and the platform keeps 22.4% of the fee by default.

**pump.fun.** Per its own documentation, its curve is "a constant-product AMM", it "charges a 1.25% total trading fee, split between the coin's creator and the protocol", and "Graduation is automatic and irreversible" ([pump.fun — Bonding curve](https://pump.fun/docs/bonding-curve)). Since 21 May 2026 creators can choose USDC instead of SOL as the paired token ([pump.fun — Fees](https://pump.fun/docs/fees)). Curve shape, fee schedule and graduation rule are set by the protocol.

pump.fun's create page, read on 23 September ([pump.fun/create](https://pump.fun/create)), offers SOL or USDC as the pair, creator rewards sent to the creator or to holders, and Mayhem mode, a creation-time switch under which, per its documentation, an AI agent trades the coin for its first 24 hours ([pump.fun — Mayhem Mode](https://pump.fun/docs/mayhem-mode)).

Read again on 24 September on its own pages. Its [fees page](https://pump.fun/docs/fees), updated 20 May 2026, gives 1.25% on the curve, 0.30% to the creator and 0.95% to the protocol; after graduation the creator fee is 0.30% up to a 420 SOL market cap, then 0.95%, falling to 0.05%. Its "Send creator rewards to Holders" option pays holders instead, and the creator gets none. Its [public GitHub documentation](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/main/docs/instructions/COIN_CREATION.md) describes creating a coin and making the creator's first buy in one transaction. pump.fun supports xStocks, including all seven that Sonata's mock stocks are priced from (SPYx, QQQx, TSLAx, MSFTx, AMZNx, METAx, MCDx); it lists no Anthropic token.

**StonkFun.** Read on 23 September ([stonkfun.xyz/launch](https://stonkfun.xyz/launch)): launches run on Raydium LaunchLab and graduate at 85 SOL raised into a Raydium pool with locked liquidity; the quote token can be one of 24 xStocks, 7 PreStocks (Anthropic, Anduril, Polymarket, Kalshi, Neuralink, OpenAI, Figure AI), Tessera, Sunrise, currencies, SOL or a custom token. An optional 1% or 3% transfer tax funds pro-rata payouts to holders in the quote token ([stonkfun.xyz/rewards](https://stonkfun.xyz/rewards)), and a platform flywheel buys back and burns the ten largest tokens by market cap, $723,764 in total when read ([stonkfun.xyz/flywheel](https://stonkfun.xyz/flywheel)). Read again on 24 September, its launch page gives a 1.25% trading fee. A Standard token pays the creator about 0.5% per trade, on the curve and after graduation, forwarded automatically. A Reward token has a 1% or 3% transfer tax on every transfer, distributed to holders, with "no separate creator fee". A dev buy of up to 75% is bundled into the launch, and a launch costs about 0.012 SOL.

What Sonata does differently: the quote asset is a stock token; each launch deploys its own DBC config reflecting the creator's chosen graduation target and fee, with fees routed to program-owned custody (§6.4); a Reward token pays holders from the trading fee with no transfer tax, where StonkFun's Reward token taxes every transfer; and the Backed token's Stock Floor (§5.5) is a burn-to-redeem reserve held by the program that the creator cannot withdraw. We did not find a burn-to-redeem floor on pump.fun's, Ember's or StonkFun's pages: StonkFun pays holders out of a transfer tax and buys back only its ten largest tokens, and Solana Compass describes StockLaunch's holder distribution as time-weighted accrual, both different mechanisms; we have not inspected StockLaunch's contracts.

Where Sonata is level or behind: a Sonata creator's 0.5% per trade on the curve matches StonkFun's Standard token and is above pump.fun's 0.30%, but after graduation StonkFun keeps forwarding about 0.5% per trade and pump.fun pays on its schedule automatically, while a Sonata creator earns from half the locked pool (0.4% of trades on it while no one else adds liquidity, §5.9) and claims it by hand. Sonata's own share, 40% of the fee (0.5% of volume), is below pump.fun's 0.95% protocol share and above Ember's default 22.4% of the fee. A first buy in the creating transaction matches pump.fun's create-and-buy and StonkFun's bundled dev buy of up to 75%. A Sonata launch costs about 0.032 SOL against StonkFun's 0.012 (§5.9). Reward token payouts are custodial on Sonata as on Ember (§5.9). Sonata does not offer more curve choice than Ember, and it is not first to pair launches with stocks.

- Stock Floor growth after graduation, which needs the Vault's DAMM v2 fee claim above
- A Backed token (StandardFloor) market on Devnet; the mode is proven only in local tests (§3)
- A lower launch cost. A launch costs about 0.032 SOL (0.0315 measured without a first buy, §5.9), against StonkFun's about 0.012 SOL; the Metaplex metadata account alone is 0.0137 SOL. Switching to Token-2022 tokens, with metadata in the mint and no Metaplex account, is planned after the hackathon

- A graduation of a market launched through the revised page; the 50/50 creator position is proven on a script-made launch (§5.9)
- A way out for DBC surplus and leftover. Each config names the Vault PDA as fee claimer and leftover receiver (§5.1), and the treasury program has no instruction to withdraw them ([`lib.rs`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata-protocol/blob/b1fcb6181babbadc6876c5911320b732ce75b62a/programs/stockroom-treasury/src/lib.rs): `init_vault`, `create_partner_metadata`, `init_treasury`, `claim`, `distribute`, `withdraw_retained`, `redeem`, and since the fourth upgrade `distribute_split`), so any surplus or leftover would be stuck in the Vault
- An in-app migrate step. On Devnet a completed curve waits until someone migrates it, with Meteora's Manual Migrator or our script (`scripts/verify-graduation.mjs`, §5.4)
- A launch signed by an extension wallet such as Phantom (the app's built-in test wallet has signed several, §4)
- A market page that fits a phone screen; it is currently wider than one
- An RPC fallback. The browser uses Solana's free public Devnet RPC (`https://api.devnet.solana.com`, [`runtime.ts:56`](https://github.com/umin-ai/sonata/blob/980cd485697ad2562e1c5054f845fef676825842/lib/treasury/runtime.ts#L56)) with no fallback, and that RPC rate-limits per IP
- Permanent storage for token profiles (Irys devnet uploads are temporary, and the first app launch's no longer displays; the bucket owner can delete S3 files, §5.6)
- Moving or revoking the upgrade authority (§6.2)
- An mNVDA Devnet mint
- Rewards-program rewards for markets not quoted in mSPY (a rewards program change; Reward tokens work in any quote token)
- Launch-time liquidity policy; liquidity allocation for any market other than `GHHFvUXd…`
- On-chain verification of per-recipient reward splits, for rewards-program rounds and for Reward token payouts alike
- The token-badge path for real stock tokens, and any mainnet deployment
- CI
- A static IP for the hosted app
- Indexing trades on graduated markets' DAMM v2 pools, and streaming transactions instead of polling the public RPC

## 11. Naming

The product is **Sonata**, renamed from the working name Stockroom on 22 September. On-chain identifiers keep the old name deliberately: the program crates `stockroom_treasury` and `stockroom_rewards`, and the vault seed `"stockroom"`.
